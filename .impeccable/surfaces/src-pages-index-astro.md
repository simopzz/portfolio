# Homepage (simopzz.com)

**Mode:** Persuade. One page: recruiters, prospective clients and engineers arrive from LinkedIn, GitHub or a CV link (often on a phone, about 30 seconds) and must leave believing Simone is a senior backend engineer who designs systems and ships whole products, then email.

- **Action:** email pedra89@gmail.com, as a mailto link in a hand-drawn box with a copy button, at the top and again at the close. GitHub and LinkedIn are secondary links.
- **Proof:** dandori (live app, public repo, decision records) and tegakko (private repo: its URL is struck through, unlinked, with "private until refactor"). Each project is a content file; its drawing comes from `src/drawings/`.
- **Copy:** the about facts read as a checklist ("Things to know"), same facts, no new claims.
- **Keep:** name, the role line "Senior Engineer — Python, Cloud, Distributed Architecture", title and description meta, the canonical simopzz.com domain.
- **Chosen by Simone in prototype rounds (2026-11-19):** Caveat Brush for hand lettering, Shantell Sans for body, highlighter underline for links; no M1 cloud on the dandori drawing; no changelog on project sections; a plain straight strike on the private repo URL; the closing email boxed like the top one, never styled as a link.
- **Anti-goals:** the previous drawing-set look (title blocks, zone grids, sheet numbering), dark terminal, invented metrics or claims outside PRODUCT.md, mascots.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The homepage is a hand-made engineering zine page: one engineer explaining their systems the way they would sketch them for a colleague. It refuses the polished hero-plus-cards portfolio and the costume of a technical artifact.

OWN-WORLD: White paper with a faint blue graph grid, black marker ink, three flat highlighter colours (yellow, pink, teal). Caveat Brush lettering for headings, asides, captions and drawing labels; Shantell Sans for reading text. Hand-wobbled SVG linework (displacement filter), boxes with uneven corners, ticked checkboxes, highlighter swipes behind headings. Yellow highlighter underline means "link"; the email is a boxed action, not a link.

STORY: The visitor reads the name and role, sees the email boxed with an arrow pointing to it, skims what Simone knows as a checklist, reads two systems sketched in marker with their purpose and links, and emails from the box at the top or the close.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left-aligned within 60rem: the name at display size in Caveat Brush on a yellow highlighter swipe, slightly rotated; the role line; a two-line hook; a hand-drawn arrow with the aside "quickest way to reach Simone" pointing into a hand-drawn box holding the email and a teal copy button; GitHub and LinkedIn below. Signature interaction: highlighters swipe in and each project sketch draws itself once on first view; under reduced motion everything is already drawn.

FORM: Hand-drawn technical zine (catalog challenger hand-drawn-zine-explainer, fused), chosen from a coded prototype in re-roll round 1, then tuned by Simone; code-led, the prototype at /proto/zine/ is the composition the build matches. seed:4db2292494eb85db
