# Homepage (simopzz.com)

**Mode:** Persuade. One page: recruiters, prospective clients and engineers arrive from LinkedIn, GitHub or a CV link (often on a phone, about 30 seconds) and must leave believing Simone is a senior backend engineer who designs systems and ships whole products, then email.

- **Action:** email pedra89@gmail.com, as a mailto link plus copy-with-confirmation. GitHub and LinkedIn are secondary.
- **Proof:** dandori (live app plus public repo plus ADRs) and tegakko (a private repo: the link is struck through with "private until refactor" until it is public). Each project is a content file with its revisions in frontmatter.
- **Copy:** the about text is rewritten as numbered General Notes with the same facts and no new claims; Simone reviews the draft.
- **Keep:** name, the role line "Senior Engineer — Python, Cloud, Distributed Architecture", title and description meta, the canonical simopzz.com domain.
- **Anti-goals:** blue cyanotype blueprint, invented dimensions or metrics, skill bars, tech-logo walls, dark terminal, any claim outside PRODUCT.md.
- **Open:** fonts are chosen at build. No print stylesheet or CV download.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The homepage is an issued drawing set. Every sheet is titled, numbered and revised, and the work is shown as real architecture drawings. It refuses the hero-plus-project-cards developer portfolio.

OWN-WORLD: White bond paper (not cream, not blue), graphite ink at three line weights, a hairline sheet border with a lettered and numbered zone grid, a ruled title block, and a revision table. Redline is the only colour: revision clouds and delta triangles, used for state and the action. The type is technical lettering: a drafting sans for titles and notes, and tabular figures in the blocks.

STORY: The visitor sees a person who designs systems, reads two real systems drawn as architecture with honest revision status, understands the notes, and emails from the title block.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Sheet 01, the cover, fills the viewport inside its border and zone grid. The name is set as the drawing title, very large, upper left. The drawing list (01 Cover, 02 General Notes, 03 dandori, 04 tegakko) works as the navigation, middle right. The title block sits bottom-right: role line, the email as the primary action with copy, GitHub, LinkedIn, sheet 01/04, rev. The signature interaction is linework plotting in once on load; with reduced motion it appears drawn.

FORM: Engineering drawing set (title block plus revision table), candidate 1 of 7 (IMPECCABLE'S PICK over the roll). seed:88dd32ffebf1b11d

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
