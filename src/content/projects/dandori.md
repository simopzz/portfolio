A meal-planning co-pilot for a few invited people, each cooking for themselves. It plans batch-cooked meals (cook once, eat three times) one grocery delivery at a time, tracks what is in the kitchen, and adapts when the week changes.

The agent proposes and the person approves, with a live trace of every agent run. It fills the supermarket cart and stops: there is no checkout.
